## 使用范围

作为美术架构的视觉讨论基准。不是生产模型、可平铺纹理、图标集或引擎截图。建筑主材质与明暗关系可用于制作对照，背景细节密度和虚化在真机按预算调整。拱形开口、烟囱和丰富植物按后续版本规划。

## 初始完整提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: art direction board for an original Bevy mobile building game, landscape presentation board.
Primary request: Design a cohesive art architecture reference board inspired by the visual qualities of Tiny Glade: cozy miniature architecture, soft warm light, rounded weathered stone, matte painted wood, subtly irregular roof tiles, gentle grassy ground, quiet and tactile beauty. Create original house shapes, motifs, and UI; do not reproduce game screenshots or branded interfaces.
Scene/backdrop: pale warm cream board, generous spacing, polished professional game art direction presentation.
Composition: six clearly arranged panels with one larger hero environment panel and smaller production panels. Hero shows a small rectangular single-story cottage with slate-blue gabled roof, warm limestone walls, original timber windows, a dirt path that creates a simple doorway through an adjacent stone wall. Keep everything miniature and playable, elevated three-quarter perspective. Remaining panels show an exploded modular door/window kit, five material spheres and color chips, a sparse landscape touchscreen editor mockup with simple house/wall/path/window pictograms, and a four-stage build transition strip showing the SAME house footprint rising smoothly from outline to walls to roof to subtle finishing details.
Lighting/mood: warm late afternoon illumination with cool soft shadows, readable contact shadow, modest atmospheric softness.
Palette: limestone cream #D8CCB4, timber #927259, roof #778894, sage grass #879D73, earth #AD9572, UI paper #F4EEDF, text #4B5148, muted selected green #6C8463.
Materials: rounded corners and subtly irregular edges but architecturally straight main structures; coarse readable surface character, restrained fine noise, no glossy plastic.
Text verbatim: only small panel labels "SCENE", "MODULES", "MATERIALS", "UI", "TRANSITIONS"; do not add other text.
Constraints: first release focus on buildings, walls, paths and windows. No people, pets, interiors, towers, water or gamepads. Compact icon-only controls support mouse and touch. No Tiny Glade logo, no copied UI, no watermark. This is a concept board, not a claimed in-engine screenshot.
```

## 定向修改提示词

初稿的 UI 面板出现了规则网格，与自由绘制方向不一致，使用以下提示词定向去除。

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the existing art direction board with exactly one change: remove all regular construction grid lines from the ground in the UI panel in the lower left. Replace them with natural continuous grass and earth matching the rest of the scene. Keep the dashed selected house outline and the compact toolbars. Preserve every other panel, house, materials, colors, labels, lighting, layout and details unchanged. The game is gridless freeform drawing. No new labels, no watermarks.
```
