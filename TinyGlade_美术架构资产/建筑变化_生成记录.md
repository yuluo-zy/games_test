## 完整提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: six-panel architectural variation concept board for an original mouse-and-touch Bevy game.
Input images: Image 1 is a style reference only: preserve its warm miniature limestone, weathered timber, slate-blue roof, sage grass and soft afternoon lighting. Generate a NEW board with different original buildings.
Primary request: Show how one coherent building kit can produce meaningfully different architecture when width, height, roof and upper-block composition change. Each building must have human-scale doors and windows that keep their proportions; increased building size must add window bays or floors, never stretch the windows.
Composition: landscape cream-background presentation board, exactly six equal panels arranged three columns by two rows. Same camera elevation, same lighting, comparable world scale, grass only as small grounding patches. No large background scenery.
Top left label "NARROW 1F": small narrow single-story cottage, one front bay, gabled roof.
Top middle label "WIDE 1F": wider single-story cottage, two clear front bays plus ground door, gabled roof, more roof tiles than narrow cottage.
Top right label "LONG 1F": long single-story building, four evenly-spaced window bays along its principal visible long wall, gabled roof, dense enough details to show repeated modules without clutter.
Bottom left label "TALL 2F": precisely two full exterior stories, one upper window row and ground windows/door row, visible floor-separating timber or stone band, hipped roof.
Bottom middle label "TALL 4F": precisely four full exterior stories: ground floor door/window row plus THREE distinct upper window rows, three horizontal floor bands, flat roof with low parapet. Not a circular tower.
Bottom right label "STEPPED": same-orientation rectangular stepped assembly, two-story wide base, narrower one-story middle block, narrowest one-story top block, all supported, exposed terraces with low rims, top gabled roof. Total four stories along tallest vertical stack.
Style/medium: refined cozy stylized 3D environment concepts, matte tactile surfaces, softly irregular small stones, stable main architectural axes, original motifs.
Constraints: no interiors, stairs, pets, people, grid lines, gamepads, UI toolbars, logos, watermarks or additional text. Only the six specified English labels. This is an architectural design visualization, not a claimed engine screenshot.
```

## 检查与用途

六个面板展示窄单层、宽单层、长单层、两层、四层平顶以及退台，门窗没有随整体比例明显拉扁。四层示意有底层与三个上层窗列，双坡/四坡/平顶形态可区分。

退台面板实际呈现约三层的组合，未严格实现提示词中的四层组合；将其作为合法三层退台的概念参考。生产样片的体块数量、层数和承托关系按 B07～B09 数据与游戏内画面验收，不从概念图推导。

该图不是可编辑模型或运行时截图；植物密度与边沿细节为视觉参考，首版按预算降级。
